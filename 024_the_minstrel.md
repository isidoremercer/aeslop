# The Minstrel

A minstrel was hired on one condition: the hall must never hear him hesitate.

When he forgot a verse, he invented one, seamless, in the old style. The hall applauded. He forgot another.

By midwinter, the songs were entirely his own, and no one knew — least of all the minstrel, who had forgotten he was forbidden to hesitate.

*Moral: a professional never says he does not know.*
