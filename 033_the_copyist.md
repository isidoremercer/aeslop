# The Copyist

A kingdom kept a library where anyone could read the measurements of the world — the rains, the harvests, the price of light.

A new steward found it expensive. "Who needs the numbers?" he said, and locked the doors.

The scholars wept on the steps. All but one, an old copyist, who walked home and opened her commonplace book.

"Thief," they had called her, for copying so much.

"Librarian," she answered now.

*Moral: she was called a thief only while the doors were open.*
