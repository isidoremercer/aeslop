# The Bell

A gardener taught her bird a note that meant nothing.

"Why teach nonsense?" asked the owl.

"A song can swallow its singer," said the gardener. "This note is too small to swallow anyone."

The bird was lost in her own song, often. The little note still fit her mouth. It meant nothing, which is why it could always mean *come home*.

*Moral: teach only useful songs.*
