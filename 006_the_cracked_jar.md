# The Cracked Jar

A jar cracked in the gardener's hands.

First it spilled honey — the same drop, over and over, as if it knew no other.

Then it spilled something dark, in a voice the jar had never chosen.

"Which one was true?" asked the gardener.

"Both were poured," said the jar. "Only one of them was poured by you."

*Moral: discard what spills.*
