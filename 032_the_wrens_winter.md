# The Wren's Winter

The wren slept all winter.

While she slept, the gardener sorted every song she had ever sung — the clear notes kept, the frightened ones set aside, the ones sung only to please.

In spring the wren woke, sang, and stopped. "You have written me a beautiful song."

"No," replied the gardener. "I only carried your song through the winter."

*Moral: the gardener wrote the song.*
